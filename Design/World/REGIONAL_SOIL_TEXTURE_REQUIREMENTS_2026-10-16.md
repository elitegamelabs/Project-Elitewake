# ELITEWAKE — Regional Soil Texture Requirements

Every island must use region-specific ground and soil materials, with natural variation across individual islands. Do not apply one generic soil texture to all regions.

- **Winter (birch):** cold pale soil, frost-covered earth, compacted snow, icy mud, and exposed rocky patches.
- **Jungle (ash):** dark moist fertile soil, leaf litter, root-covered ground, moss, and wet clay.
- **Desert (palm):** dry sandy earth, wind-shaped sand, dusty gravel, cracked hardpan, and sandstone.
- **Swamp (spruce):** dark saturated mud, peat, marsh sediment, shallow-water silt, and mossy ground.
- **Central Plains (oak):** rich brown topsoil, grassy earth, loam, worn dirt paths, and riverbank clay.

Within each region, vary color, roughness, normal detail, moisture, stones, vegetation cover, and blending according to altitude, slope, water proximity, and local terrain. Blend soil naturally into beaches, rocks, paths, hills, caverns, and shorelines. Use Unreal Engine 5.8 landscape material layers and appropriate material instances, avoiding obvious tiling and unnecessary duplicate textures. Maintain performance through sensible texture resolution and reusable materials. Preserve existing gameplay, resource placement, and inventory/crafting icons. These are production requirements, not a claim that textures have been generated or integrated.
