# Explorer's journal: twelve entries, DRAFT for owner approval (written October 28, 2026)

Status: **DRAFT text, not in the game.** Voice: a survivor's brief field notes, warm and a little wry, never grim. Each entry is
tied to something the player can already find. The tone follows the owner's light-story direction: no new lore facts beyond what the
design notes already hold (five regions, a shipwreck start, a port).

1. **Washed Up.** "Salt in my hair, sand in my boots. The boat is gone but the beach is kind. First job: find something to eat and
   something to burn."
2. **The Hatchet.** "A good edge changes the day. Oak falls slower than I thought; the stump will be a stool tonight."
3. **Stone and Strike.** "Struck a rock until it gave up a spark. I have decided that patience is a tool like any other."
4. **Berries.** "Red ones and blue ones. I ate the blue ones. I am writing this, so that is a good sign."
5. **Night Falls.** "The island is louder in the dark. Insects, surf, something rustling that I have decided is a rabbit."
6. **The Cave.** "A cool breath from the hillside. There are webs across the mouth. Whatever lives there has been busy and has more
   legs than I like. I will come back with a light and a better plan."
7. **Glitter in the Dark.** "Red, blue and green stones set in the wall, as if someone had hidden the sky down here. It takes a
   proper pick to free them. I will need to earn that pick."
8. **A Bite.** "Something fast and many-legged. I got away with a bruise and a lesson about caves."
9. **The Port.** "Lamps on the water at dusk. Someone built this place to be found. I am not as alone as I thought."
10. **Open Water.** "The horizon has an edge, but a ship has none. The sea is a road if you can read it."
11. **Cold Wind.** "Four islands out, the air turned sharp. Snow on the hills. The bears here are the size of my plans."
12. **Where the Map Ends.** "The map is only as large as my footprints. I am filling it in one cautious step at a time."

Notes for the owner: entries 6 to 8 refer to cave spiders and entry 7 to crystals, both now in the game; entry 11 refers to Winter
bears. Which entry unlocks on which event (first level, first cave visit, first bite, first port) is not decided here.
