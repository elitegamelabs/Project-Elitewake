Coverage: six DA_Animal definitions present in the tree; eight CREATURES keys in build_fish.py; five approved additions (Bear, Lion, Giant Crocodile, Elephant, Spider). This assignment audit covers 19 kinds; two additional already-approved habitat animals below bring the full handoff to 21 kinds, not 21 verified production rigs. ## Timing, state and provenance
Use species-specific animation notify/contact events; server confirms the hit, clients play the corresponding motion/contact. No damage from audio alone, no duplicate replicated/local contact sound. Idle calls need variation, cooldowns and distance checks; locomotion follows actual foot/water contacts. A dead/hidden/resting creature should not continue idle calls. Reuse existing sound library routes, attenuation and voice limits; no new balance values imposed by this matrix.
Existing references/models are not proof of complete idle/walk/sprint/action animation coverage. Audit each clip and imported sound before implementing. Generated SFX remain timing drafts with provenance, not authentic field recordings.

## Research and source/code distinction
- [Marine Megafauna Foundation anatomy guide](https://marinemegafauna.org/species/manta-rays/guide/anatomy): mantas have no functional sting. build_fish.py currently assigns MantaRay attack_name='sting'; this conflict is documented, code unchanged. Delegated choice: anatomically correct non-stinging art; implementation owner reconciles the attack.
- [NOAA giant manta overview](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/giant-manta-ray): filter-feeding identity; no predatory bite audio is proposed.
- [Smithsonian Asian elephant](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/asian-elephant): elephant communication supports rumbles/trumpets. This source informs the audio family, not the game's selected African-style visual identity or habitat.
- [San Diego Zoo lion](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/lion), [brown bear](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/brown-bear), [tarantula](https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/tarantula): anatomy/behavior references. Proposed game audio slots are authored design choices, not claims that each species vocalizes in every slot.
