Legend
- Status (repository evidence): EXISTS (built and Play-checked), PARTIAL (a foundation exists), NEW (nothing yet).
- Priority: ESSENTIAL (the core voyage loop or a stated owner direction needs it), RECOMMENDED (deepens a working loop),
  OPTIONAL (long-term; kept, with its dependencies).
- Complexity: S (days), M (one to two weeks), L (several weeks), XL (a season of work; split before starting).
- Every feature also carries the universal requirements in Part A, which are not repeated under each subfeature.


PART A - UNIVERSAL REQUIREMENTS (apply to every feature below)
-------------------------------------------------------------
A1 Server authority: clients request; the server validates distance, ownership, cost, cooldown and state, then applies
   and replicates. Inventory, currency, reputation, crew, damage, ownership and saved state are never client-written.
   Retries are idempotent (a request id per transaction).
A2 Persistence: every new persisted object gets a stable id, an owner scope, a payload version, a migration path for
   older saves, missing-reference behaviour and duplicate detection (CLAUDE.md 8). Single player uses UEWSurvivorSave /
   the world state; hosted games use the server's world save.
A3 Performance on the GTX 1660 Super (1080p Low, about 4.5 GB VRAM): anything simulated far from players sleeps or is
   aggregated; decoration never blocks movement or is gatherable (CLAUDE.md 9.6: quality tiers change looks only);
   new textures at 1024-2048 with mips; Niagara effects scaled by the Effects quality group; measure frame and GPU time
   in the benchmark mode (-EWBenchmark) before and after.
A4 Input: every action on keyboard/mouse and controller, listed on the Options' Controls page (EWControlsList.h),
   verified by a Play check; the controller-only check (verify_controller_only_play.py) extended for new actions.
A5 Presentation: 1740-1760 naturalistic realism; placeholders labelled; owner visual and audio review before acceptance;
   every external or generated asset in ASSET_LEDGER.csv first.
A6 Evidence: a Play check per feature (single player, plus listen server and client where networked), save/load
   round trip, controller path, and the fast Python suite; status words from CLAUDE.md 1.5.

FEAT-001 DYNAMIC MARITIME WEATHER                                   Part 8: P8-02 (storms), P8-01 (currents, see FEAT-003)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evidence: UEWWindSubsystem (Ships/EWWindSubsystem.h) owns a replicated wind yaw that drives sails, flags and the compass;
AEWSnowfall shows Winter snow under the replicated climate; UEWDayNightSubsystem runs a 90 + 90 minute day; the
soundscape (UEWSoundscapeComponent) mixes wind by region. No rain, storm, lightning, fog bank, wave-height or weather
state exists; the ocean surface is the Water plugin's with fixed waves.
Integration: one server-owned weather state per region (and per open-sea zone) feeds the existing wind subsystem (gust
strength, veering), the ship (heel, speed, sail strain), the climate (cold, wetness from FEAT-009), the soundscape and
lighting (cloud cover, exposure), wildlife (shelter-seeking, FEAT-008) and the port (warnings, FEAT-010).
Unreal: UEWWeatherSubsystem (UWorldSubsystem, server authority) with FEWWeatherState {Kind, Intensity 0-1, WindBoost,
Visibility, Precipitation, Lightning chance, NextChange}; replicated through AEWGameState; UEWWeatherProfile Data Assets per
region (allowed kinds, odds, durations, seasonal weights); AEWWeatherFront actor for a travelling storm cell (map marker,
sound, Niagara); Water plugin wave parameters driven by intensity (Gerstner amplitude/choppiness, read the same on every
machine for fair sailing); Volumetric Cloud and Exponential Height Fog parameters; Niagara rain, spray, lightning;
UEWLightningComponent for strikes (server picks targets).
Assets: rain/spray/snow-drift/sand-streak Niagara systems; lightning bolt and flash; storm cloud material instances;
wet-surface material function (puddle and darkening layer on decks, timber, sand); thunder, rain-on-canvas, rain-on-sea,
wind-howl and rigging-strain sounds (3+ variations each); weather icons for the HUD and forecast UI.
References: EW_REF_FEAT-001_StormAtSea, EW_REF_FEAT-001_RegionalWeatherBoard, EW_REF_FEAT-001_WeatherIcons.
Multiplayer: the server rolls weather and strikes; clients interpolate intensity; lightning and damage events are
multicast with a seed so effects match; a player's ship damage from weather is applied by the server only.
Persistence: the current weather per region, the active fronts (position, heading, remaining time) and the forecast
seed are saved with the world; a load resumes the same storm rather than re-rolling it.
Performance: rain and spray only within 60-80 m of the camera, GPU particles capped by the Effects tier; one lightning
light at a time; fog and clouds through existing volumetrics (tier-scaled); no per-raindrop collision.
Dependencies: none to start (wind and climate exist); FEAT-009 wetness, FEAT-003 sail damage and FEAT-008 behaviour
consume it later.
Complexity: L (XL with full wave coupling). Priority: RECOMMENDED overall (Wave B); storms that change sailing are
ESSENTIAL to the voyage pillar ("weather or threat changed the plan", CLAUDE.md 2).
Conflicts: CLAUDE.md 2.1 readability first: storms must keep the horizon and landing cues readable (9.3); weather must
never block a port or trap a swimmer; the Water plugin's wave changes must not break buoyancy checks or the sea caverns.
Acceptance: each region shows its weather kinds in Play; a storm visibly raises waves and heel and changes speed; the
weather survives save/load; two clients see the same weather and lightning; frame time measured in a storm on the 1660.
