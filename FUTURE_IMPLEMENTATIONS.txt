What this file is
-----------------
The single list of everything still to build for gameplay and the world, in the order to build it, with what already
exists and what the owner has already decided for each piece. Read it at the start of every session after CLAUDE.md.

- CLAUDE.md stays the rulebook (how to work, evidence, the world bible). This file is the work list that follows from it.
  If they disagree, the owner's newest instruction wins, then CLAUDE.md; fix this file to match.
- When something here is built and verified, move it to "Done" (part 7) with its commit, in the same commit as the work.
  When the owner decides something listed under "Waiting on the owner" (part 6), write the decision where it belongs.
- Each new feature is its own commit (owner, November 7), so every change stays separate.
- Status words are CLAUDE.md's: Implemented, Verified, Owner visual review pending, Owner audio audition pending, Accepted,
  Deferred, Blocked. Nothing here is done because it is written down.

   C:\ELITEWAKE\WorldActors_<date>.

   never give a gameplay advantage.
7. Visual target: high-definition naturalistic realism, 1740-1760 maritime frontier. Placeholders are labelled as such.
8. Every third-party or generated asset gets an ASSET_LEDGER.csv row before it is used.
9. Decide details yourself and explain plainly; ask the owner only for destructive, outward-facing or money decisions,
   and for the open decisions in part 6.


PART 2 - WHERE THE GAME STANDS (November 7)
-------------------------------------------
Built and verified in Play unless marked otherwise. Visuals and audio are placeholders awaiting owner review.

Front end: title splash and menu; Single Player opens the Freeport choice (whole world, all 20 islands, five Freeports);
  Resume Game only after playing before; Create New Survivor; character creator (male/female, 6 presets, 68 sliders,
  8 choice lists, up to 6 composed tattoos; mouse, keyboard, controller); Set Sail; the loading screen ChatGPT designed;
  a new survivor starts at the chosen Freeport.
World: 20 islands (4 per region: Plains centre, Winter north, Swamp south, Desert west, Jungle east as built), one Port
  per region at its village, Saltreach Freeport as the working hub; caves, sea caverns (Plains3, Desert3, Jungle3),
  climb-outs on every coast, landmarks, regional soils, 231,505 forest trees and 177,567 bushes, Winter ice sheets,
  three open-ocean reefs, a 1.5-2 km deep sea floor with seamounts, day/night (90 minutes each), Winter snowfall,
  climate and body temperature. World Partition streaming for ground cover.
Resources: wood, stone, fibre, berries, water; crystals, obsidian, coal in caves and Winter mountains; island specialty
  ores (copper, iron, sulfur, salt, amber, bog iron) and gathered goods (clay, sand, reeds, peat, resin, mushrooms, dates,
  coconuts, wild fruit, spices, vines) on their planned islands; smelting iron and copper ingots; gunpowder.
Wildlife: 38 species, 509 animals, roaming, grazing, resting, three temperaments; cave and swamp spiders (rideable);
  elephants push trees over; reef sharks and manta rays; fish in the starter pond and the sea. All rigid placeholder models.
Survival: hunger, thirst, sickness, cooking at a campfire, death, same-region respawn, 30-minute death bag, needs HUD,
  skills (Wayfarer constellation), stats, level 50 cap, journal.
Tools and combat: stone hatchet, pickaxes (wooden, metal), waterskin, torch, bare-hand punches (first and third person).
Building: Build Mode with foundations, walls, doorways, windows, doors that open, ceilings, roofs, three staircases;
  saved in single player and on the server.
Ships: raft, dinghy, sloop, schooner, brigantine, Cavalier (built November 7, not yet sold or moored); fittings, helm,
  per-sail control, crew-dependent helm, sailhands and gunners for hire, anchor and capstan, cargo hold and weight, wake
  and bow waves, cannons with sponge-powder-shot-ram loading, recoil, gunports with covers, gunners who lead a moving
  mark, hull health, holes, leaking, bailing, sinking into wreck crates, flags, railings, ship surgeon, ship ownership and
  Company sharing, ships saved.
Economy: Copper purse (starts at zero), Port merchants buy and sell, restock, Freeport shipwright, crew masters.
Multiplayer and saving: hosted/listen games, direct IP join (F10), rejoining players keep their survivor, Company screen,
  survivor, ship, world resource, structure, death bag and wreck saves.
Tools for development: Play-check runner and ~150 Play checks, native automation tests, benchmark mode and the GTX 1660 test.

    - Note: a new survivor now starts at the chosen Freeport; decide with the owner whether a Plains start still opens on the

4.2 WORLD AND ISLANDS (Phase 2)
    - Per-island evidence set (CLAUDE.md Phase 2): 1 km offshore views, landings, landmarks, day/night/weather, profiling.
    - Stream the islands themselves with HLOD for distant ones (today only ground cover streams); measure on a GTX 1660 Super.
    - Weather: rain, storms, Winter blizzards, Desert sandstorms, poor visibility; storms affect sailing. Only Winter snowfall
      exists. Night climate.
    - The Swamp river (CLAUDE.md 5.5) sized and sailed for the Cavalier: measure her turning circle, then a coast-to-coast voyage.
    - Bases on hills and cliff shelves: supported foundations and terrain-marked shelves instead of the flat-ground limit.
    - Gathered specialties regrow; uses for clay, peat, reeds and vines; pitch from resin for ship repair.
    - Felled forest logs saved while lying (standing felled trees already are).
    - Landmarks readable at 1-2 km; safe and difficult landings per island; freshwater on every island where it belongs.
    - Map: drifting grey clouds over the map art (owner, October 22); no fog of war (owner, November 3).
    - Decorative PCG separate from persistent resources; authored items never move when decoration is regenerated.

4.3 WILDLIFE
    - Realistic rigged models with walk, run, idle, attack, hit, death and sounds, species by species (Phase 5). Today every
      animal is a rigid lofted model. The generated (Meshy/Scenario) statues were removed November 7 as unusable.
    - Recognisable attacks and warning behaviour; bites synced with sound; large predators need preparation or a crew.
    - Food chains: predators hunt prey, not always the player; prey flees unless cornered; feeding, drinking, territory.
    - Taming: the Tames screen and the caps (25 per player, 75 per Company) exist; taming itself is not built.
    - Marine life by depth and habitat: schools, shore creatures, predators, large animals. The manatee stays out of the game.
    - Elephants: other obstacles, crack sounds, push animation, never built pieces; navigation round fallen logs.
    - Spiders: turning, hit, death and web-spinning animations; a seated rider pose.
    - Saving the wildlife the game needs (not every decorative animal).

4.4 SURVIVAL AND CHARACTER
    - Fall damage on solid ground; stamina-limited climbing.
    - Clothing with warmth and protection; humidity and heat in Desert, Jungle and Swamp; freezing in Winter.
    - More skill constellations (Hunter, Cook, Builder were proposed October 28, not approved): only with the owner's
      approval, UI, save migration and tests. Respec rule still to be decided.
    - Blocking, dodging, stamina and reach in land combat.
    - The explorer's journal entries (12 were drafted October 28 for owner approval; not in the game).

      brigantine; large: Cavalier; a larger yard does not build smaller ships.

    - Merchant stock, restock and transactions saved (Phase 4); regional goods and prices per Port; buying in quantity.
    - Retry-proof purchases, sales, crew hire and repair (no duplicates under lag or reconnect).

    - Crew wages, crew limits per hull, dismissing and losing hands.
    - Copper -> Silver -> Gold exchange in use (500 Copper = 1 Silver, 30 Silver = 1 Gold exist in the purse).
