# ELITEWAKE — Tree Foliage and Winter Snow Direction
Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Owner-requested visual direction; implementation and Unreal validation pending.

## All non-palm trees
All non-palm trees across all islands should have full, dense, natural-looking foliage, with species-appropriate leaves or needles, canopy shapes, branch structure, and natural variation. Avoid thin, sparse, artificial-looking crowns. Palm trees retain their own realistic fronds and are excluded from this foliage instruction. Maintain appropriate LODs and gameplay performance.

## Winter islands
Winter-region trees, including the designated birch species, must have dense foliage with **partial snow coverage**. Accumulate snow naturally on upper and outward-facing foliage and branches while leaving some leaves or needles visibly exposed beneath and between snow patches. Avoid uniformly white trees or flat white texture overlays. Vary snow coverage by tree, branch orientation, and exposure. Preserve convincing snow depth, lighting, shadows, wind movement, and tree silhouettes. For deciduous birch, retain seasonal plausibility while following the owner's requested visible foliage.

## Acceptance checks
- Non-palm trees in every region have visibly dense, species-appropriate foliage.
- Winter trees show a believable mixture of snow-covered and exposed foliage, not solid-white crowns.
- Wind, collision, harvesting, and gameplay performance remain functional.
- Verify the appearance in Unreal Engine at close and gameplay viewing distances.
