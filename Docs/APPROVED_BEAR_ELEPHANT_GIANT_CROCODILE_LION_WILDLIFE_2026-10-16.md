# Approved additional wildlife — October 16, 2026

Owner confirms these additional animals for ELITEWAKE: **bears, elephants, giant crocodiles, and lions**. No other unusually large or legendary creatures are approved by this decision. Preserve the existing approved roster.

## Proposed region placement (subject to owner review)
- **Bears:** Northern Winter forests and mountain foothills; realistic growls, roars, bites, swipes.
- **Elephants:** Warmer open terrain in the Central Plains, preferably on appropriate non-starter islands; trumpeting, rumbling, trunk motion, charges, tusk attacks; do not force growling or biting behavior.
- **Giant crocodiles:** Southern Swamp rivers and wetlands; lurking, swimming, powerful jaw bite, splash, hiss/bellow, death-roll if gameplay supports it. Giant size is explicitly owner-approved, but exact scale remains to be decided.
- **Lions:** Open grassland/savanna pockets of the Central Plains; roar, snarl, bite, pounce and paw swipes. Avoid spawning high-threat predators directly in the beginner safe area.
